#ShieldLab

> Turning STEM concepts into creatures, stories and unforgettable animations

## What is ShieldLab?

ShieldLab is an educational animation project exploring mathematics, physics and computer science through visual storytelling

The goal is to make difficult ideas impossible to forget

---

## Projects

### 🎬 Animations
Manim-powered explanations of STEM concepts

### 🧠 Proofs
Visual explorations of mathematical reasoning

### Shield Creatures
Original creatures inspired by scientific and mathematical ideas

### 🌌 Experiments
Interactive simulations and generative art

---

## Philosophy

People rarely remember equations

People remember stories

ShieldLab combines both
